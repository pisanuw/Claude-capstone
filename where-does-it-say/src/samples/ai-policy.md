# Collaboration and AI Policy

This policy applies to all labs, projects, quizzes and exams in CS 142.

## Working with other students

You are encouraged to discuss ideas, error messages and approaches with classmates. The code you submit must be written by you (or by you and your registered partner, where a lab allows partners). Do not look at another student's code for the same assignment, and do not share yours.

Projects are individual work unless the project handout says otherwise.

## Generative AI tools

Generative AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Copilot are allowed on labs 1 to 4 for explaining error messages and concepts, but not for writing the code you submit. From lab 5 onward, and on both projects, you may also use AI tools to write code, provided you cite the tool and include your prompts in a file named `AI-LOG.md` with your submission.

AI tools are not allowed on quizzes or exams.

If you are unsure whether a use is allowed, ask the course staff before submitting.

## Citing help

Any help that shaped your submission, from a person, a website or an AI tool, must be cited in a comment at the top of the file. Citing help is never penalized; failing to cite it is treated as an academic integrity violation.

## Violations

A first violation of this policy results in a zero on the assignment and a report to the Office of Student Conduct. A second violation results in failing the course.
